1. PROJECT MISSION & OPERATIONAL CONTEXT
================================================================================
Lenskart provides Home Try-On (HTO / DLC) optical services across pan-India regional hubs.
Field Executives (FEs / Optometrists / Refractionists) travel to customer residences equipped with:
- Clinical diagnostic tools (Trial frame, lenses, JCC, blue/yellow ophthalmic torches)
- Near-vision test cards & telescoping distance vision standees
- Frame trays with optical frames & sunglasses
- Official Lenskart uniform polo with visible employee photo ID card

During each visit, the FE uploads mandatory compliance photos into the Locus dispatch mobile app:
1. Customer verification selfie with OTP
2. Diagnostic equipment tray laid out flat
3. Telescoping standee deployed at seated eye level (never taped to walls)
4. Active blue & yellow penlights operational test
5. Uniform & badge compliance

"Locus Auditor" is the central enterprise web application that ingests Locus appointment spreadsheets, auto-allocates photo inspection tasks to active auditors, allows fast pass/fatal reviews, escalates SLA breaches to leadership, and aggregates daily performance into corporate warning emails.

2. END-TO-END SYSTEM PIPELINE & DATA FLOW
================================================================================
[Step 1: Dispatch Spreadsheet Upload]
  - Uploaded in Upload Tasks (`UploadPage.jsx`) via SheetJS (`.xlsx` / `.csv`).
  - Tasks parsed and automatically inspected for image attachments (`has_images = true`).

[Step 2: Auditor Roster & Workload Auto-Allocation]
  - Handled in `AutoAllocationRoster.jsx`.
  - Shift attendance checked (`AVAILABLE`, `ON_LEAVE`, `HALF_DAY`, `TRAINING`).
  - Auditable tasks (those with photos) distributed equally across available auditors.
  - Tasks without photos are separated so auditors only spend time reviewing real images.

[Step 3: Systematic Photo Auditing]
  - Handled in `AuditModal.jsx` / `App.jsx`.
  - Auditor reviews uploaded images against SOP criteria.
  - PASS: Task saved as `PASSED` in Supabase.
  - FATAL: Task saved immediately via `tasksApi.failAudit`:
      • `audit_status = 'FATAL'`
      • `email_notified = false` (QUEUED for daily batch, NO popup interrupts the auditor!)
      • Defective equipment flagged into `replacement_orders` table.

[Step 4: Real-Time D+1 SLA Tracker]
  - Handled in `DPlusOneEscalationDashboard.jsx`.
  - Calculates a 24-hour countdown strictly from the upload/allocation timestamp (`task.created_at` or `task.allocated_at`), preventing false SLA alerts from historical dates.
  - Overdue audits escalated to Business Head (BH) & General Manager (GM) (dynamically resolved from database users).

[Step 5: Automated Nightly Cron Job 1 - Manager Daily Digest (20:00 IST)]
  - Route: `/api/cron/daily-digest` (declared in `vercel.json`: `0 20 * * *`).
  - High-level KPI digest for Area Managers (Hub/Cluster level) and Regional Managers (Territory level).
  - Identifies 7-day rolling repeat offenders (FEs with >= 2 fatal strikes in 7 days).
  - AMs/RMs can view digests in-app and click "Acknowledge & Sign-Off".

[Step 6: Automated Nightly Cron Job 2 - Fatal-Only FE Warning Engine (20:30 IST)]
  - Route: `/api/cron/daily-fe-fatal-digest` (declared in `vercel.json`: `30 20 * * *`).
  - Aggregates today's fatal audits at the FE level.
  - STRICT FILTER: Only FEs with >= 1 Fatal strike receive an email (FEs with 0 fatals receive NO email, preserving 95%+ email quota!).
  - Routing: TO: FE, CC: direct Area Manager, CC: Regional Manager.
  - Instructs FE to reply-all with written explanations before their next shift.
  - Updates Supabase: `email_notified = true`, `email_sent = true`, `email_sent_at = NOW()`.

Feature 1: Streamlined Photo Auditing Flow (Zero Email Modal Interruption)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- File: `src/App.jsx` (`handleFatalAudit`) & `src/api/client.js` (`tasksApi.failAudit`)
- Auditor action: Auditor opens a visit with photos, reviews checklist.
- When clicking "FAIL (FATAL)":
  1. Calls `tasksApi.failAudit({ taskId, taskData, failedReasons, remarks, criteriaStatus, replacements, immediateEmail: false })`.
  2. Saves directly to Supabase `tasks` table with `audit_status = 'FATAL'` and `email_notified = false`.
  3. Registers any broken items in `replacement_orders`.
  4. Immediately advances to the next task in the review queue with a clean toast notice.
  5. ZERO popup modals or email drafts to slow down the auditor!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Feature 2: Fatal-Only FE-Level Aggregated Warning Email Engine
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Helper File: `src/utils/dailyDigestHelper.js` (`aggregateFeFatalDigests`)
- Endpoint File: `api/cron/daily-fe-fatal-digest.js`
- Core Rules:
  1. Only FEs with fatalCount >= 1 are returned. FEs with 0 fatals receive NO email.
  2. Resolves direct Area Manager (AM) via `reporting_manager_email`, `reporting_manager_id`, or hub match.
  3. Resolves Regional Manager (RM) via `regional_manager_email`, `regional_manager_id`, or regional pool.
  4. Tri-Party Corporate Thread Routing:
     - TO: Field Executive (`feEmail`)
     - CC: Area Manager (`amEmail`)
     - CC: Regional Manager (`rmEmail`)
  5. HTML Template Features:
     - Prominent Red Non-Compliance Strike Notice header.
     - Summary pills: Total Audited, Total Passed, Total Fatal Strikes.
     - Specific failure cards with failure reasons, auditor remarks, and deep-link to inspect photos.
     - Mandatory Supervisory Action Block:
       • FE must reply-all with written explanation before next shift.
       • AM must perform physical diagnostic kit inspection before next dispatch.
       • RM records strike in compliance registry (>= 2 strikes triggers suspension).
  6. Updates Supabase tasks: `email_notified = true`, `email_sent = true`, `email_sent_at = NOW()`.
